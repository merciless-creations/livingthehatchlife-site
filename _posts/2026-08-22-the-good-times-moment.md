title: "The Night the Whole Bar Started Singing"
categories: [origin-story, steinhatchee]
tags: [good-times, music, marina]
---

The first place I ever stayed in Steinhatchee was Good Times Motel and Marina.

That first night, I was sitting in the restaurant bar on the water when a girl walked in with a guitar, sat a couple chairs down, propped her foot on the bar, leaned the chair back, and started singing.

<!--more-->

The next thing I knew, the whole place was singing with her. That was the moment I knew I had found something special.

Good Times is mentioned here as a personal memory, not as a sponsorship or affiliation.
