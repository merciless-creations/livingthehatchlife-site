Scooter is the wide-eyed spark of the crew — the little scallop who reminds everyone why they fell in love with the Hatch Life in the first place. Every day on the water is his best day ever.

He is optimistic, friendly, brave, loyal, and a little clumsy in the best possible way. Scooter is the audience's way into the world: scalloping, Gulf water, family, friendship, and the joy of finding magic in small moments.

> "Best day ever!"
