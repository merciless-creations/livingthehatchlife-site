Bad Bonnie is the crew's grumpy rival who desperately wants to win and, deep down, wants to belong. He cheats only with silly shortcuts, usually loses, and never truly means harm.

He adds comic tension, eye rolls, and heart. Bad Bonnie teaches that losing gracefully matters, and that grumpy on the outside does not always mean bad on the inside.

> "I almost had it."
