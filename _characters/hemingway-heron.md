Hemingway represents the timeless connection between the people, wildlife, and waterways of Steinhatchee. He has watched generations of fishermen, scallopers, boaters, and families enjoy the river and the Gulf.

He is patient, observant, sophisticated, humorous, and a little dramatic — the old soul of the crew.

> "The water always tells a story."
