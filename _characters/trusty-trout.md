Trusty is the calm compass of the crew — the guide who reads the water, reads the weather, and reads people. He says little, but when he speaks, everyone listens.

He brings quiet wisdom, dry humor, patience, and practical fishing know-how to the Hatch Life world.

> "Fish are where the bait is."
